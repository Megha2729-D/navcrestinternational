Navcrest International is a coco peat exporter from India, established to bring quality coco peat to global markets. Founded by Jawahar Maruthamuthu In Coimbatore, a region known for its strong coconut-growing and coir industry, the company was built with a clear vision to connect customers worldwide with quality coco peat from India.

We source our coco peat directly from our manufacturing unit, giving us greater control over quality and consistency while keeping us closely connected to the production process. This direct sourcing approach allows us to serve global buyers with consistent products and dependable supply.

Guided by our motto, “Where Products Meet Precision,” we are committed to building lasting relationships with our customers through care, reliability, and dependable service.

Driven by a strong focus on product consistency, operational efficiency, and global supply capability, Navcrest International operates as a reliable coco peat supplier in India and a trusted partner for international markets. We are committed to supporting bulk requirements through disciplined execution and continuous improvement. Along with product quality, we emphasize building long-term trust with our customers, as we believe strong partnerships are essential for sustainable business growth.

By providing dependable customer support and year-round assistance, Navcrest International aims to build strong, long-term relationships with its clients. As a reliable coco peat supplier in India and coco peat exporter, our commitment to consistent product quality enables us to serve diverse international markets with confidence.

Our Journey

From Coimbatore to Global Markets

We cater to a wide range of global markets, including Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Africa, supplying quality coco peat sourced from India and processed to meet the requirements of different horticultural applications.

Supported by our manufacturing unit and a streamlined supply chain, we operate as an efficient coco peat supplier, offering coco peat products to meet different requirements. As an experienced exporter, we ensure smooth coordination, transparent documentation, and hassle-free shipping for global buyers.
