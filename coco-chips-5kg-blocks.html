Product Overview

Coarse Growing Medium for Better Aeration & Drainage

Produced from processed coconut husk chips, Coco Chips 5 KG Blocks provide a coarser growing medium with good aeration and drainage. Their compressed form allows for convenient storage and transportation before being hydrated for use.

Coco Chips can be used alone or blended with coco peat for greenhouse cultivation, nurseries, potting mixes, and substrate applications. Particle size and other specifications can be adjusted according to buyer requirements.
