Product Overview

Compact Coco Peat for Easy Gardening

Compact and easy to handle, 650G Coco Peat Bricks are designed for gardening, nursery, propagation, retail, and smaller-scale horticultural applications. The compressed bricks expand after hydration, providing loose coco peat for use as a growing medium.

Their convenient size makes them suitable for retail packs, garden centres, home gardening, and smaller-scale horticultural applications. EC, pH, moisture, packaging, and other specifications can be customized according to buyer requirements.
