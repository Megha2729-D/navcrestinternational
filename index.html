ABOUT US

Navcrest International -
Coco Peat Exporter from India

Navcrest International is a coco peat exporter from India, established to bring quality coco peat to global markets. Founded by Jawahar Maruthamuthu In Coimbatore, a region known for its strong coconut-growing and coir industry, the company was built with a clear vision to connect customers worldwide with quality coco peat from India.

We source our coco peat directly from our manufacturing unit, giving us greater control over quality and consistency while keeping us closely connected to the production process. This direct sourcing approach allows us to serve global buyers with consistent products and dependable supply.

Guided by our motto, “Where Products Meet Precision,” we are committed to building lasting relationships with our customers through care, reliability, and dependable service.

5 KG Coco Peat Blocks

Premium coco peat blocks made from natural coconut fibre, offering excellent moisture retention, aeration, and a clean growing medium for plants.

5 KG Custom Mix Blocks

Custom-blended coco substrate designed to meet specific growing requirements, providing the right balance of moisture, drainage, and aeration.

5 KG Buffered Coco Peat Blocks

Buffered coco peat processed for improved consistency and growing performance, making it suitable for professional horticulture and controlled cultivation.

Coco Chips 5 KG Blocks

Ready-to-use coco substrate grow bags designed for efficient plant growth, offering excellent root aeration, moisture management, and convenient cultivation.
