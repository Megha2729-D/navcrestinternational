Coco Peat Products for Global Growing Applications

Navcrest International supplies a range of coco peat and coir-based growing media designed for different horticultural and cultivation requirements. Our product range includes compressed coco peat blocks, custom mix and buffered blocks, coco chips, 650G bricks, and ready-to-use flat bed grow bags.

Each product can be supplied in different grades and specifications to suit the intended application and market requirements. From bulk compressed blocks to ready-to-use growing media, we focus on consistent specifications, practical packaging, and export-ready supply for buyers worldwide.
